# Personal Grilling

Pressure-test the named requirement before implementation. Keep ordinary
conversation separate from this explicit interview mode.

Start from the user's existing decisions and actual open question. Investigate
facts with bounded read-only checks. Offer a concrete recommendation and two
or three materially different choices when the options are known; use a
concrete example when an abstract question would be difficult to answer.

Ask one material decision at a time, following prerequisite decisions before
asking downstream questions. Wait for an explicit answer; silence is not a
choice. Bind a short answer to the complete option that was shown. Do not ask
again about a settled choice unless new evidence changes it.

For multi-turn continuity, reuse a named plan or decision owner. If none exists
and settled choices need a durable record, keep one compact task-owned note:
current scope, decisions with their user answer or evidence, open questions,
and next material decision. Avoid transcripts, scoring tables, and duplicate
ledgers. Reread that owner after compaction or handoff.

Stop when no unresolved choice could materially change the outcome, scope, or
acceptance. Summarize the agreed design as well as the decisions, and disclose
remaining assumptions. Do not generate new questions to fill a coverage model.
Planning or implementation follows only the user's corresponding request;
respect next-step authority already given rather than demanding it again.

Read `references/source-notes.md` only when maintaining provenance.
