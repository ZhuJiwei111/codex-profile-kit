# Wayfinder

Use this only when uncertainty about the route, rather than implementation
volume alone, makes the effort too large to settle directly. A clear feature
needs no decision map.

Start from the agreed outcome, scope, evidence, and current user decisions.
Choose the largest unresolved prerequisite and resolve it through a concrete
user choice, bounded primary-source research, or an authorized small prototype.
Keep decisions separate from the implementation tasks they may later produce.
Do not create external accounts, resources, credentials, or persistent jobs as
implicit research steps.

Use the existing plan or decision owner. If the user requested a saved map,
record the goal, consequential open decisions and dependencies, settled choices
with evidence, and the next material question. Do not create an issue tree or
second ledger merely to follow an upstream template. External tracker writes
and delegated research still require their matching authority.

Work prerequisite decisions first and preserve settled answers across turns.
Stop when the route is clear enough to produce the requested specification or
implementation scope. Summarize the agreed design and actual remaining choices;
continue related authorized work in the same chat rather than requiring a
handoff or fresh session at an arbitrary boundary.
